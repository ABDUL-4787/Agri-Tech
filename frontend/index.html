How We Grade

We use our own simple rules. They look at size and damage.

1. Reference Coin

A ₹10 or ₹5 coin on the cloth gives the scale in millimetres.

2. Two Photos per Batch

Photo A (stem up) and Photo B (stem down) reveal both sides.

3. Count of Out-of-Grade Fruit

Grading is by number of bad fruit in the sample.

4. Limitations

Inside rot and soft fruit cannot be seen in a photo.

Our Grading Rules

These rules are ours. They are not AGMARK. They are not government rules. They may change.

Grade A, B and C are our own names.

Grade Max Defect % Shape Tolerances
Grade A < 1% Round (≥ 0.90) Max 5% out-of-grade
Grade B ≤ 3% Regular (≥ 0.80) Max 10% out-of-grade
Grade C ≤ 8% Fair (≥ 0.65) Max 10% out-of-grade
Reject > 8% Irregular Severe defect or decay
